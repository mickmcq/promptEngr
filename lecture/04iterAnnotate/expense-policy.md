Posit Expense Policy

1. Introduction and Purpose
This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures for employees to incur business-related expenses and seek reimbursement. The purpose is to ensure all company expenses are necessary, reasonable, and properly documented. 

2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Posit. 

3. General Principles

    All expenses must be directly related to company business.
    Expenses should be reasonable and employees are expected to exercise fiscal responsibility.
    Employees must submit expenses within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
    All expenses must have a corresponding receipt or proof of purchase. 

4. Expense Categories and Guidelines

4.1 Travel Expenses

    Flights: Economy class is the standard. Business or first-class travel requires prior approval from a department head.
    Accommodation: Employees should book standard hotel rooms at a reasonable rate. If a nightly rate exceeds $250, a justification is required.
    Ground Transportation:
        Reimbursable: Rideshare services (e.g., Uber/Lyft), taxis, and public transportation for business purposes.
        Personal vehicle: Mileage can be reimbursed at the standard IRS rate.
    Meals: Reasonable meal expenses for individual travel are reimbursable. Team meals for business purposes are also covered. 

4.2 Office and Supplies

    Reimbursable:
        Office supplies necessary for the job, such as pens, notebooks, and printer ink.
        Ergonomic equipment with prior approval from HR.
    Not Reimbursable:
        Personal items, including snacks or coffee for personal consumption.
        High-end electronics without prior approval. 

4.3 Client and Team Entertainment

    Guidelines: Any entertainment expense must be for a clear business purpose.
    Prior Approval: Requires prior approval from a manager for any single expense over $100.
    Documentation: Receipts must include the business purpose and names of the people present. 

5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following items are examples of non-reimbursable expenses:

    Fines, late fees, or traffic violations.
    Personal travel, including family expenses.
    Loss or damage of personal property.
    Gift cards or other gifts without explicit approval. 

6. Reimbursement Process

6.1 Submission

    System: All expense reports must be submitted through the company's designated expense management software (e.g., Expensify, Concur).
    Documentation: A clear, legible image of the receipt must be attached to each expense line item. 

6.2 Approval and Payment

    Approval: All expense reports must be approved by the employee's direct manager.
    Payment: Reimbursement is typically processed with the next scheduled payroll cycle after approval. 

7. Policy Violations
Violations of this policy, such as submitting false or inaccurate expense reports, may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. 

8. Questions
For any questions regarding this policy, please contact the Finance department.
